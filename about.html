About the Hotel

A good night's rest,
off the Kasoa Road.

Nikops Royal Hotel sits on the Tuba-Kokrobite Road at Ngleshi Amanfro, between the coast and the city.

What to expect

A day at Nikops Royal.

Arrive

Easy to find, off Tuba Junction

We are on the Tuba-Kokrobite Road, just off the Kasoa Road at Ngleshi Amanfro. If you are navigating by Ghana Post GPS, the digital address is GS-0356-3338. Check-in opens at 12:00 PM.

Settle

Your room, en-suite

Every room has its own private bathroom with a standalone shower — no shared facilities, whichever room you book. The whole property is non-smoking, which keeps the rooms fresh.

Swim

The outdoor pool

Open to all guests, with loungers on the terrace and bar service if you want it. It is the thing guests mention most often, and the reason a lot of them come back.

Eat

Restaurant and bar, on site

You do not need to head back out for dinner. The restaurant serves through the day and the bar stays open into the evening, both open to guests and their visitors.

Wake

Breakfast, three ways

À la carte from the morning menu, a Continental spread, or a Full English if you have a long day ahead. Check-out is at 12:00 PM, so there is no need to hurry through it.
