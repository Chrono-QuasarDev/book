Experience the Escape

Seven ways to lose
track of the day.

Nothing here is scheduled unless you want it to be. Wander in any direction and the resort rearranges itself around you.

  • The pool that forgets where the sea begins Heated to 29°C, open from first light until the last star.
  • An hour that undoes a year Cliffside treatment rooms with the windows left open.
  • Dinner that runs long on purpose Two kitchens, one cellar, no rush between courses.
  • Sand you will likely have to yourself Six hundred metres of private shoreline, attended all day.
  • Rooms built around the view, not the bed Every suite faces water. No exceptions, no courtyard rooms.
  • The lounge hour, every evening at seven Cold negronis, live double bass, the sun doing the rest.
  • Out past the headland by noon Skippered day sails, sea caves, and a lunch basket aboard.

Dining & Culinary

Two kitchens, one obsession.

Everything is caught, grown or pressed within forty kilometres of the gate — then treated with as little interference as the chef can manage.

Nine courses, twenty-eight seats, and a pass you can watch from every one of them. Chef Inês Caldeira cooks the bay itself — line-caught turbot, sea fennel from the dunes, and olive oil pressed by the family two coves north.
