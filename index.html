Around the Hotel

Everything you need,
and nothing you don't.

A straightforward, comfortable place to stay on the Tuba-Kokrobite Road — with a pool to come back to at the end of the day.

  • The pool, open through the afternoon Loungers, shade, and somewhere to cool off after the drive.
  • Rooms kept simple and clean Every room en-suite, with a private bathroom and shower.
  • The restaurant, open to guests and visitors Breakfast, lunch and dinner without leaving the grounds.
  • A cold drink at the bar Open into the evening, for guests and their visitors alike.
  • Breakfast, three ways À la carte, Continental, or a Full English — your choice each morning.
  • Private bathroom in every room Standalone shower, hot water, fresh towels daily.
  • Evenings on the terrace The best hour of the Accra day, and it costs nothing.

Restaurant & Bar

The Restaurant

Breakfast, three ways

Choose how you want to start: à la carte from the morning menu, a lighter Continental spread, or a Full English if the day ahead calls for it. Lunch and dinner are served in the same room, and the kitchen is happy to work around what you need.

Explore the Area

Close to the coast,
close to the city.

Kokrobite's beaches are down the road, and central Accra is a straightforward run up the N1.

01 — Coast

Kokrobite Beach

Straight down the Tuba-Kokrobite Road. Drumming, seafood, and one of the best-known stretches of sand near Accra.
